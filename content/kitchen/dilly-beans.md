Recipe originally from [Everyday Shortcuts](https://everydayshortcuts.com/dilly-beans/). On the list to make with Skylar. I plan to use this recipe to find out if I like Dill.

### Staple Ingredients
- 1/2 cup white vinegar
- 1 tsp dried dill
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1/2 tsp celery seed
- sea salt

### Special Ingredients
- 1/2 lb fresh green beans
- 1 clove garlic, crushed or maybe minced? recipe is unclear

### Instructions
- wash and trim green beans. Cut so that they fit in the jar
- boil 1 quart water and 1/2 tsp salt. Blanche beans for 30 seconds
- ice the beans 🔫
- add beans to mason jar with garlic
- simmer 1/2 cup vinegar, 1/2 cup water, 1/2 tsp salt, 1 tsp sugar, 1 tsp dill, and 1/2 tsp celery seed
- add to beans until covered. Seal mason jar
- refrigerate 24 hours before serving
