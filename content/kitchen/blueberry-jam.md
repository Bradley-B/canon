Recipe originally from [The Little Epicurean](https://www.thelittleepicurean.com/blueberry-refrigerator-jam/). Sunday swap????

### Staple Ingredients
- 3/4 cups sugar

### Special Ingredients
- 3 cups blueberries
- 2 tbsp fresh lime juice or lemon juice
- 1 tsp lime zest or lemon zest

### Instructions
- over medium heat, cook blueberries, sugar, lime juice, and lime zest for 15-25 min until the blueberries have broken down and the mixture has thickened
- recipe says: the jam is done when it falls off a frozen spoon slowly in one thickened mass
- transfer jam to a jar, then let it cool with the lid off. Cover & store in the fridge for up to 2 weeks
