Recipe originally from [Joy Food Sunshine](https://joyfoodsunshine.com/the-most-amazing-chocolate-chip-cookies/).

Makes 36 cookies. I made this recipe for work a few times, with one person saying "this is the best chocolate chip cookie I've ever had".
It was later revealed that he was on a diet and hadn't eaten any butter in the past 6 months.

The recipe is very particular about the following items:
- spoon and level the flour in a dry ingredient measuring cup
- do not over-bake the cookies, they will not look done when you take them out of the oven. Take them out when lightly browned
- use a cookie scoop for portioning. I don't own one of these

### Staple Ingredients
- 1 cup salted butter, softened (modification: add 1/4 tsp of salt per stick for unsalted)
- 1 cup white sugar
- 1 cup light brown sugar, packed. The recipe for light brown sugar is 1 cup white sugar and 1 tbsp molasses.
- 2 tsp pure vanilla extract
- 2 eggs
- 3 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1/2 tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp sea salt

### Special Ingredients
- 2 cups chocolate chips

### Instructions
- onion on 375
- line baking sheets with parchment paper
- in medium bowl: mix dry ingredients: flour, baking soda, baking powder, salt.
- in large bowl: cream together butter and sugar. beat in eggs and vanilla
- add dry ingredients to wet ingredients, mix until combined
- add chocolate chips
- portion into 2-3 tbsp dough balls
- bake 8-10 minutes
- cool
