Recipe originally from [Taste & Tell](https://www.tasteandtellblog.com/sweet-and-sour-chicken) by way of Skylar. Legend has it that this is the
only thing she can eat without triggering an allergic reaction.

Chefs say: "I'm talking so yummy I’ve had this recipe saved since sophomore year of high school when I took my around the world cooking class".

### Modifications
- I suspect this would be good if you replaced the chicken breasts with chicken thighs. I will report back if I try this.

### Staple Ingredients
- Chicken
    - 1 cup cornstarch
    - 3 eggs lightly beaten
    - 1/4 cup vegetable oil
    - Salt & Pepper
- Sauce
    - 1 cup sugar (!)
    - 6 tablespoons ketchup
    - 3/4 cup apple cider vinegar
    - 1 1/2 tablespoons soy sauce
    - 1 1/2 teaspoons garlic powder

### Special Ingredients
- 2 1/2 lbs boneless skinless chicken breasts

### Instructions
- Prepare Sauce
    - sugar, ketchup, cider vinegar, soy sauce, garlic powder
- Chicken
    - cut chicken into bite-sized chunks, season with salt & pepper pepper pepper
    - onion on 325
    - dredge chicken in cornstarch, then egg. Cursorily brown chicken.
    - Transfer to baking dish 
- Add sauce to chicken and bake for ONE HOUR stirring every 15 min
- Serve with rice
